### What I work on

- **Multimodal LLMs**: training, ablations and post-training (SFT, GRPO, MPO) for vision-language and audio-language models
- **Serving and efficiency**: vLLM, quantization (AWQ, GPTQ, GGUF), multi-node training with DeepSpeed and FSDP
- **Speech**: Whisper-based English-Kazakh speech translation and real-time subtitles
- **3D and world models**: Gaussian Splatting, WebXR, browser-based 3D worlds
